Selected research

  1. DATE 2026Best Paper

    INSPIRE: In-Sensor Compressed Weight Retrieval for Enhancing ViT Efficiency at Edge

    Compressed model weights are retrieved inside the sensor, so Vision Transformers run efficiently on edge devices.

  2. WACV 2026Oral

    Unified Alignment Protocol: Making Sense of the Unlabeled Data in New Domains

    A way to learn from unlabeled data that still works when the test data comes from a new domain.

  3. ICCV 2025

    MixA: A Mixed Attention approach with Stable Lightweight Linear Attention to enhance Efficiency of Vision Transformers at the Edge

    Mixes linear and quadratic attention in any Transformer. Vision Transformers run up to 30% faster.

  4. CVPR 2025

    DeepCompress-ViT: Rethinking Model Compression to Enhance Efficiency of Vision Transformers at the Edge

    A compression method that adds to pruning and quantization. Models become 15 to 20 times smaller.

  5. CVPR 2024

    Deep-TROJ: An Inference Stage Trojan Insertion Algorithm through Efficient Weight Replacement Attack

    Shows that a Trojan can be inserted into a model at inference time by replacing a small part of its weights.

  6. ICCV 2023

    SSDA: Secure Source-Free Domain Adaptation

    A new attack surface: backdoors survive when a model adapts to a new domain. Our defense cuts attack success to under 5%.
