eBay ML Challenge 2024: vehicle parts compatibility

A solution that extracts vehicle parts compatibility (fitment) data from eBay listings.

  • Data preparation. Converted raw HTML to text, then cleaned the text with Qwen2.5-32B-Instruct to remove noise and irrelevant information.
  • Model development. Fine-tuned two open-source LLMs (Mistral-Nemo and Qwen2.5-14B) with LoRA and k-fold training on the labeled data.
  • Ensemble. A majority vote combines the predictions of the k-fold models.

eBay ML Challenge 2023: named entity recognition

A model that extracts and labels named entities in eBay item titles.

  • Teacher and student. A teacher-student training approach with XLM-RoBERTa and ELECTRA models.
  • Semi-supervised learning. The teacher makes pseudo labels on unlabeled data, and uncertain samples are filtered out.
  • Training. K-fold training and an ensemble combine the predictions of the k-fold models.

Teaching an agent to play Ms. Pac-Man from pixels

Deep reinforcement learning · UC Riverside · 2022. Two changes make DDQN and Dueling DDQN play Atari Ms. Pac-Man better from raw pixels.

  • Reward shaping. Separate “no progress” (−1) from “life lost” (−200). This lifts DDQN by 53.6%.
  • Self-supervised helper task. The network also predicts whether the ghosts are scared and how many power pills are left. The labels come for free from the reward stream.
  • Result. The helper task improves both algorithms (+30.4% and +29.8%). The best agent averages 3,156 points.
